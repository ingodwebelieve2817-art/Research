Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when registering a customer or business account. This includes name, business category, location, phone number, email address, and payment information.

When matching customers with service providers, we process location and service request parameters to automatically dispatch campaigns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services, including matching customers with relevant local businesses and dispatching messaging campaigns.

We will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Phone numbers are utilized solely for automated outreach requested by providers.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your information is stored in secure database instances hosted by our infrastructure providers (Supabase). We employ industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and access controls to secure your personal records.

Note: This policy meets general regulatory standards. Users are encouraged to obtain independent legal advice prior to deploying this platform commercially.
